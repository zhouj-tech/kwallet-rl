## 9. Remaining reviewer risks

1. The feedback rule (BFP) remains the strongest controller; the paper
   already frames learning as an audit with null/negative results reported,
   but reviewers may still ask when learning wins — addressed honestly via
   the discussion paragraph, not hidden.
2. One failed $k{=}24$ set seed (532) and asymmetric zero-shot transfer
   (4433 at k=6->24 vs 43733 at k=12->6): disclosed in Table 3, prose, and
   limitations; cross-k evidence at $k{=}24$ rests on two surviving seeds
   for the sensitivity number (headline keeps all three seeds).
3. Single environment family / one generator (12 regimes), homogeneous
   wallets, fixed fee, no lookahead: enumerated as limitations.
4. Transfer table has no paired inference across architectures (flat MLP
   cannot be deployed off-diagonal by construction); the claim is
   operational deployability, not superiority, and the caption says so.
5. Reference depth is now 23 entries spanning the five target areas; it is
   not padded, but a PCN-domain reviewer may expect more Lightning-specific
   routing citations if page space were free.
