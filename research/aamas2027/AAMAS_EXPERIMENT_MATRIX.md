## Hypotheses and paper relevance

**A:** A compact structured policy can retain or improve Money relative to flat JA-PPO under the original training protocol. The test is a direct contrast, not a guarantee that every factorized implementation improves. This supports the central representation story and checks whether old differences persist beyond repeatedly inspected streams.

**B:** The settlement input may or may not help at either confirmed capacity; existing C800 and C1200 intervals span zero. A fixed head with the embedding zeroed tests the input signal more directly than SC versus the smaller IFAC. This can justify a capacity-specific mechanism statement; a null or negative result supports making conditioning a qualified refinement. Four additional runs are enough to complete the requested five-seed comparison, not to prove absence of an effect.

**C:** A simple state-feedback controller is a strong comparator and may remain superior to SC-FAC. The question is empirical; do not write a hypothesis that presumes RL superiority. This is essential to avoid a misleading applied-control claim and costs no training.

## Exact rule definition and fairness

Implement the following mathematical specification independently in the original environment as a NEW AAMAS baseline. The separate local diagnostic supplies exploratory context only; do not copy collaborator BFP code or use its numbers. From the current observed state, let U be usable wallets, b_i their balances, and x the current transaction value. Choose settlement s as the lowest-balance member of U with b_i≥x; ties by wallet index. If none exists choose no settlement. Among U excluding s, choose the lowest-balance wallet with b_i < 0.5(C/k) as flush f; ties by index. If none exists choose no flush. Submit this pair to E0, which executes its normal flush-before-settle transition. When x is oversized, the rule may still flush an eligible wallet. Do not add an early-return no-op, future information, extra flushes, threshold search, or different delay.

A flush restores full C/k after the original delay; the 0.5 threshold is not a partial refill. The rule only uses the same balances, availability, and current transaction available to the agent. Its control logic avoids settling a wallet it simultaneously flushes, but the environment remains unchanged for all methods. Call it **best-fit, balance-threshold full-refill (BF-T0.5)** consistently. The .5 is a remaining-balance trigger, never a refill fraction; the original environment restores a flushed wallet to full C/k after F. Explain that .5 was specified in the prior local diagnostic after old-test inspection; NEW12 is the confirmation pool.

## Statistical reporting contract

The primary replicate is the trained policy seed, conditional on the frozen test pool. Report mean and sample SD across five seeds; a 95% t interval for a paired mean difference uses t_(.975,4)×SD(difference)/sqrt(5). Report all five paired points, not only significance stars. Seed-label pairing is a design choice and is not a claim that RNG trajectories match across architectures.

Use two-sided paired t tests with effect sizes and unadjusted CIs, clearly labeled. Apply Holm correction within the fixed, separately stated scientific families: A has six contrasts (SC−JA, IF−JA, SC−IF at two C); B has two full−zero contrasts; C has two SC−rule contrasts. Also disclose all families together; do not selectively report a favorable family or call unadjusted intervals simultaneous. For historical A0, report the requested contrasts and define its retrospective family explicitly; the retained audit's eight-test comparison across the broader recovered four-capacity archive is a separate retrospective sensitivity analysis, not the current two-capacity test family or a preregistration.

The deterministic rule has one result per fixed pool/C. Do not duplicate that result five times and present zero SD as evidence of perfect robustness. Its differences from the five learned policies quantify training-seed variability conditional on that pool. If an episode bootstrap is included as a cheap secondary analysis, resample paired episodes within each regime and label it as stream uncertainty; do not treat 2,400 episodes as 2,400 independent trained agents. No additional simulation is needed for that calculation. Retain per-episode outputs for later scrutiny.

No early stopping based on a p-value, no changing seeds after looking at results, no test-based checkpoint selection, no post-hoc threshold fitting. Failed jobs may be retried only with the same declared configuration and a recorded failure reason. A CI spanning zero means inconclusive, not equivalence. Five seeds will not resolve every small effect.
